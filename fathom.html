Core Discovery

The Decomposability Law

Fathom measures computational irreducibility, not accuracy or length. Two responses with identical factual content produce different scores based on whether the computation is modular or integrated.

The Law
answer = A₁ + A₂ + A₃ (independent parts) → depth ≈ 0
Decomposable: list of facts, sequential steps, independent modules
answer requires A₁ × A₂ × A₃ (all together) → depth ≈ 0.4+
Irreducible: causal web, integrated synthesis, interdependent mechanisms

A perfectly correct algebra solution scores 0.0000 because each step is independently computable. A causal explanation of how exercise affects neurotransmitter regulation scores 0.4749 because every mechanism depends on the others. Depth measures structure, not quality.
