Empirical Calibration

The Fathom Scale: 0.00 to 0.57

Ten cognitive task types, scored on Gemma-2-2B at temperature 0.0. The scale is monotonically ordered by integration requirements: pure computation scores zero, meta-logical self-reference scores highest.

Discovery

Task-Specific Stochastic Resonance

A small amount of noise (T=0.3) increases reasoning depth for concept-level integration tasks but decreases it for formal-precision tasks. The same pattern appears in biological neural systems.

Method

How Fathom Works

Three steps from prompt to depth score.

01
Instrument
Pass a prompt through the transformer. At each of 26 layers, record which SAE features activate and their magnitudes.
02
Map
Build the activation distribution: where does computation concentrate? Early layers? Mid layers? Output-proximal?
03
Score
Compute normalized depth from the distribution. Lower = deeper reasoning. Higher = surface recall. One number. Sub-3 seconds.
